Welcome to my GitHub profile. I am a researcher focusing on the intersection of **Quantum Systems, Quantum Optics, and Machine Learning**. I develop high-performance numerical simulations to model quantum phenomena and apply data-driven approaches to optimize quantum control and error correction.

### 🔬 Research Interests
* **Quantum Optics & Open Quantum Systems:** Modeling light-matter interactions, decoherence channels, and cavity quantum electrodynamics (cQED).
* **Numerical Simulations:** Developing robust solvers for time-dependent Schrödinger and Master equations using Python (QuTiP), C++, and Julia.
* **Quantum Machine Learning:** Leveraging deep learning and reinforcement learning to discover optimal quantum control protocols and accelerate state estimation.
